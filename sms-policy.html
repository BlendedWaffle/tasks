Tasks: SMS Terms & Privacy

Operated by Josh Campbell, sole proprietor. Last updated October 22, 2026.

What this service is

Tasks is a private, personal task-management tool. The owner texts the Tasks phone number to add items to his own to-do list. The service may reply with a short confirmation that the task was added. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public.

How to opt in

Text TASKS to +1 (971) 458-1546 to receive task confirmations from Tasks.

Msg frequency varies (about one confirmation per task added). Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to cancel. Terms & Privacy: blendedwaffle.github.io/tasks/sms-policy.html

After texting TASKS, you’ll receive: “Welcome to Tasks! You’ll get a confirmation each time you text a task to this number. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out. Terms & Privacy: blendedwaffle.github.io/tasks/sms-policy.html Reply Y to confirm.” After replying Y, you’ll receive: “You’re all set! Text any task to add it to your board.”

The only recipient of messages is the owner, who set up the service and opts in from his own phone. Replies are sent only in response to a message the owner sends. No phone numbers are collected from anyone else, and no messages are sent to third parties.

Message frequency and costs

Message frequency varies with usage, typically one confirmation per task added. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out and help

  • Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. You’ll get one final confirmation, and no further messages will be sent.
  • Reply START to resume messages.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact YOUR_EMAIL_HERE.

Privacy

Message content is used only to create tasks on the owner’s private task board. Phone numbers and message content are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing or any other purpose. Mobile opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties. Data is stored with the service’s hosting and database providers solely to operate the task board.
